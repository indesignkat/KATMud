  PROVENANCE - READ FIRST

  This file is NOT an in-game help scrape. Unlike bards.txt,
  elementals.txt, knights.txt, mercs.txt and warders.txt, which are
  verbatim copies of what the mud prints, 3S publishes no GMCP help
  for bladesingers. Everything below was reconstructed from a single
  wire capture:

      logs/gmcp_20260830_123436.log
      3s, character Din, glevel 73, 2026-08-30, 12:34-12:59

  Treat it as observed behaviour of one character over 25 minutes, not
  as a specification. Field meanings marked (inferred) are our reading,
  not the mud's word.

  What arrives

  Bladesingers are the plain three-package shape, not the ten-package
  shape the bards get:

  Guild.State      Blur and portal recharge, the nekra and sokra
                   pools, guild XP, and the two panic settings.
                   Sent every 2s tick, in combat or NOT - see the
                   cadence section, it does not go quiet when idle.
  Guild.Info       Regeneration rates, rune totals, guild level.
                   Sent ONCE at login and then only on change; in
                   25 minutes it arrived exactly once.
  Guild.Extra      Declared, never sent. Not one message in 25
                   minutes. Either blades do not populate it or
                   nothing in the session triggered it.

  Do not read Core.Supported as a guild manifest. Din's is
  byte-identical to the elemental capture's - it lists Merc.*, Craft.*
  and Mud.Status too. It is a static server-wide list and says nothing
  about which packages your guild actually fills.

  Guild.State

    reset             blur recharge, percent
    portal_reset      portal recharge, percent
    nekra, nekra_max  the prompt's NE: pool
    sokra, sokra_max  the prompt's SO: pool
    gxp               guild experience
    gtnl              gxp to next guild level
    gxp_last_round    gxp gained last round (0 out of combat)
    evade_cmd         panic command       (Din: "rejuvenate")
    evade_hp          panic trigger hp    (Din: 400)
    evade2_cmd        second panic command
    evade2_var        what the second one watches (Din: "health")
    evade2_level      second trigger level (Din: 4000)
    guild             always "bladesinger"

  Guild.Info

    glevel                            guild level
    gxp is NOT here - it is in Guild.State
    hp_regen, hp_max_regen            hp regen and its cap
    sp_regen, sp_max_regen            sp regen and its cap
    nekra_regen, nekra_max_regen
    sokra_regen, sokra_max_regen
    runes_prepared, runes_inscribed   lifetime rune counters
    guild                             always "bladesinger"

  The blur and portal timers

  This is the useful part, and it is exact.

  Both counters climb by a fixed step, one step per combat round, and
  wrap 100 -> 0. Across 474 consecutive samples there was no variation
  in either step:

    reset         2/27 % per round   ->  1350 rounds for 0->100
    portal_reset  1/4  % per round   ->   400 rounds for 0->100

  A round is 2.000 seconds, confirmed two ways: portal_reset wrapped
  twice, 799.952s apart against 800s predicted, and Char.Combat.rounds
  advanced at 2.011 s/round. So:

    blur    1350 rounds = 45m 00s
    portal   400 rounds = 13m 20s

  A blur wrap predicted twenty minutes in advance from the 2/27 step
  landed at 12:55:27.538 against 12:55:27 predicted.

  Because the step is exactly 100/1350, the percentage is lossless -
  the server is dividing an integer round counter, and you can recover
  it:

    rounds until blur refill    = (100 - reset)        * 13.5
    rounds until portal refill  = (100 - portal_reset) * 4

  How long a blur lasts: 48 rounds

  The counter is also the cleanest way to time a blur, because it ticks
  once per combat round - so the percentage it advances between the cast
  and the fade IS the duration in rounds, with no clock involved.

  Cast shows up as the hpbar's B-count dropping; the fade is the line
  "The reflective shadow surrounding you disintegrates into nothingness."
  Both blurs measurable in the capture:

    cast 56.5926%  ->  fade 60.1481%   = 3.5556%  = 48.0 rounds
    cast 76.4444%  ->  fade 80.0000%   = 3.5556%  = 48.0 rounds

  3.5556% is 48/1350 exactly. Wall time was 95s and 96s, but that only
  agrees because both windows were continuous combat (confirmed by the
  player) - a blur does not tick down while idle, so ROUNDS is the real
  unit and seconds are only a convenience.

  So eight charges cover 384 of the cycle's 1350 rounds, and a blur
  cannot be recast while one is still running.

  Two things the wire does NOT tell you

  1. The charge count. GMCP carries the recharge percentage but never
     the "three of eight". That number is text only - the info readout
     and the hpbar's B:n/m field. The cap of 8 is from that text, not
     from the feed.

  2. Whether blur is currently active. It appears in the info box's
     "Guild Effects:" line and nowhere in GMCP. Guild.Extra, the
     obvious home for it, never fires.

  Cadence, and where the general doc does not apply

  Guild.State is a metronome: 2.002s median interval, and across 25
  minutes not one gap exceeded 5 seconds.

  Combat is irrelevant to it. 368 Guild.State messages arrived after
  the last Char.Combat packet, through twelve minutes with no combat at
  all, and the blur cycle wrapped in the middle of them. The heartbeat
  tracks the tick, not the fight.

  It does stop when you go idle, and the cutoff is TWENTY MINUTES.
  Measured: the last player input was 12:44:51 and the last Guild.State
  arrived at 13:04:51, +1200.0s later, to the tenth of a second.

  The "20 seconds" in bards.txt is a TYPO for 20 minutes (per the
  player, and our measurement agrees exactly). knights.txt already says
  20 minutes. Read the cutoff as 20 minutes everywhere - do not build a
  client around a 20-second silence that will never happen.

  The idle clock runs from your last INPUT, not from your last fight or
  your last packet. Two packages behave differently either side of it:

    Char.Vitals, Char.Combat   went quiet ~150s after the last input,
                               but that is just combat ending - they
                               are change-driven, and nothing was
                               changing. Not the idle timer.
    Comm.Channel.Text          keeps arriving long past the cutoff
                               (13:13, +28 min). Chat is driven by
                               other players, so it is not yours to
                               go idle.

  This matters for anything scheduling blur: the reset deadline is
  wall-clock and runs whether or not you fight, but a blur charge is
  spent in combat ROUNDS, so sitting idle does not burn charges. The
  two clocks do not run together.

  Core drift worth knowing (both differ from gmcp.txt)

  Char.Vitals    sends all seven keys on every message with no `full`
                 key at all, and does NOT carry the guild, qp and
                 qp_required that gmcp.txt promises. Observed keys:
                 hp, maxhp, sp, maxsp, enc, coffin, coffin_max.
                 enc is encumbrance as a percent of capacity.
  Char.Combat    behaves as documented: attacker, attacker_hp,
                 rounds, target, and one empty snapshot per combat
                 end (four fights, four empty snapshots).

  For client authors

  The first message of each package carries full: 1 and every key;
  everything after is changed keys only, with no full key. Merge, do
  not replace. Guild.State arrived as one message; the elemental
  capture's split across page/pages, so be ready to append.

  See also: gmcp.txt, and katmud_lib/blade.py BLUR_RESET_SECS, which
  is derived from the numbers above.
