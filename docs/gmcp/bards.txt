--======================================================================--
-- Bard Info: GMCP
--======================================================================--

  GMCP is a way for your client to receive guild information as
  structured data instead of as text on your screen.  If your client
  supports it, it can draw its own karma and voice bars, list your
  songs as buttons, or show your active effects in a side panel.

  Nothing here is new information.  Everything the guild sends is
  something you can already see with hp, gs, gs2,
  info, songs, spells or powers.

  Turning it on

  Your client subscribes by sending:

      Core.Supports.Set ["Guild 1"]

  That gets you every package below.  If your client only wants one,
  it can name it exactly, for example ["Guild.State 1"].

  What gets sent

  Guild.State      Karma, voice, smiles, and whether thunderous
                    bellow and defensive harmony are ready.
                    Sent every combat round.
  Guild.Song       The song you are performing and how many
                    rounds are left.  Sent every combat round.
  Guild.Actives    Your active effects, the same ones the
                    hp bar shows in [[ brackets ]].
                    Sent every combat round.
  Guild.Progress   Guild level, GXP, GXP to spend, and progress
                    to the next level.  Sent about every 15 seconds.
  Guild.Skills     Your eleven skills with their current value,
                    maximum, and training cost.  Sent about every 15
                    seconds.
  Guild.Info       Career, title, guild age, when you joined,
                    who recruited you.  Sent about every 15 seconds.
  Guild.Assets     Donations, songbook colours, and your panic,
                    bandage and mystic guardian settings.  Sent about
                    once a minute.
  Guild.Songs      Every song you can currently perform.  Sent
                    about every five minutes.
  Guild.Spells     Every spell you can currently cast.  Sent
                    about every five minutes.
  Guild.Abilities  Every ability you can currently use.  Sent
                    about every five minutes.

  Your hit points and spell points are not in these packages.
  They are sent separately as Char.Vitals, along with your quest
  points, so that there is only ever one source for them.

  For client authors

  The first message for each package carries full: 1 and every
  key.  After that only changed keys are sent, with no 'full'
  key at all, so you must merge each message into the picture you
  are holding rather than replacing it.  When nothing changes,
  nothing is sent -- silence is normal, not a lost message.

  Nothing at all is sent, for any package, while you are disabled,
  dead, or idle for more than 20 seconds -- the same conditions that
  blank your hp bar.  A quiet connection under those
  conditions is expected, not a dropped message.

  A few values use -1 where the game displays a word instead of a
  number:

    Guild.Song       rounds -1    the song has a variable duration
    Guild.Songs      duration -1  varies;  0 means instantaneous
    Guild.Skills     cost -1      the skill is maxed
    Guild.Abilities  cost -1      the cost varies
    Guild.Actives    rounds 0     no duration is shown for that effect
    Guild.Assets     bandage tier 1 minor, 2 normal, 3 major
    Guild.Assets     pct 1        the value is a percentage, not hit points

  Song voice costs are given as a percentage of your maximum
  voice, which is exactly what the songs command prints -- as a
  decimal number, not a whole number.

  A long list may arrive split across several messages carrying
  page and pages.  When the same key appears on more than one
  page, append the pieces together -- do not overwrite.
