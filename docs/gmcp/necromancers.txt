Necromancer GMCP feed (3s)
==========================

Capture: logs/gmcp_20260903_172942.log (895 packets), against
logs/mip_20260903_172943.log and the interleaved session log
logs/dru-20260903.log. Character Dru, Reaper subguild.

Necros are the first guild ported that had NO guild MIP tag at all. Their
data rode the mud-wide FFF feed plus THREE TYPED READOUTS the player had to
ask for - `powers`, `gs` and `inv`. So nothing here enters GMCP_SUPERSEDES;
the port is additive by construction, and what it retires is the typing.

Ten packages. FOUR ARE UNDECLARED - Core.Supported listed only

Guild.Powers - the prize
------------------------

Paginated, 4 pages: page 1 carries the scalars and no rows, pages 2..n
carry `memorized` rows of {name, amount, tier}. `amount` is remaining uses
(the number the player tracks); `tier` is the globe cost per use, equal to
the `(n)` the powers table prints for all 25 powers - verified, unused.

Buffered until the last page lands. _necro_powers_apply reads a power's
ABSENCE as "depleted to 0", so publishing a half-arrived sweep would zero
every power not yet sent.

FIVE COMPLETE SWEEPS in the capture while `powers` was typed only TWICE -
so three arrived unprompted, at a dead-regular 4 repower_pct ticks apart.
All five carried all 25 powers, and the last agrees with the `powers` table
the player typed at the end of the session, 25/25. test_necro_gmcp replays
both.

NO TRUNCATION. This was the expected risk - Guild.State cuts elementals at
32 of 48 fields on an alphabetical page boundary, and NECRO_POWERS has 34
entries - but powers do not ride Guild.State. Pagination is the Viking
sweep shape, not the elemental truncation shape.


Why POWER_FIRE was deleted, not kept
------------------------------------

necro.POWER_FIRE mapped a cast's message to a power and subtracted ONE. It
existed only because there was no live feed. It is now deleted, and the
reason is correctness, not tidiness.

A power has MANY rotating flavour messages, and POWER_FIRE knew one.
`dream` fired nine times in the capture, across four distinct lines, and
the user supplied a fifth the capture did not contain:

  3  (VI)You grasp the earth with your skeletal talon and heal yourself.
  3  (VI)Silvery strands of mystic darkness sooth your soul.
  2  (VI)Red shadows of darkness dance around your soul, reviving it.
  1  (VI)Mystic shadows of death and darkness heal your mind and body.
  -  (VI)You make contact with the darkness and heal yourself.

Nine lines, and the sweeps show nine uses (dream 890 -> 888 -> 886 -> 883
-> 881). So the rate IS one use per line here - but POWER_FIRE held only
the fourth message, so it would have logged 1 of 9. The variant set is
open-ended; collecting it by hand was never going to converge.

THE `(VI)` PREFIX (user, 2026-09-03: "see the pattern?"). Every dream
message carries it, and it is the only stable thing about them. In the
whole session there are exactly NINE `(VI)` lines, all dream, matching the
nine uses 1:1, and no other roman-numeral prefix appears anywhere.

So a prefix match WOULD be a reliable dream detector where the prose never
could be. It is still not worth building: Guild.Powers is exact for all 25,
and an estimator shadowing an authoritative feed can only disagree with it.
And critically, `(VI)` does NOT generalise - drain's line carries no prefix
at all ("The energized mist returns to you with a revival of life energy!"),
so the pattern misses the one power the player most needs accurate.

What `VI` MEANS is unknown. It is not the globe cost (dream is tier 3) and
not the number of usages spent (nine lines, nine uses).

(An earlier draft of this file blamed the undercount on multi-use casts.
That was wrong for this capture; the four-message split is the actual
cause. The multi-use mechanic below is real and independently breaks a
subtract-one estimator, but it is not what happened here.)

GAME FACT (user, 2026-09-03): `dream` takes a count of trailing z's -
`dream zz` consumes two usages on one line - and the higher your tier the
more you can spend at once. It also appears to clamp to what you need.

So maintaining POWER_FIRE means hand-collecting every flavour message for
every power AND solving the multi-use problem. That is the treadmill
Guild.Powers removes: it reports all 25 exactly, unprompted.

drain fired once and moved 868 -> 867, so the estimator was 1:1 there - but
that is a single sample, its full message set is UNKNOWN, and whether drain
accepts the same z-suffix is UNCONFIRMED.

The player's priority (2026-09-03) is drain, not dream: running out of
dream costs attack frequency, running out of drain is probably death. That
argues FOR the deletion. Guild.Powers reports all 25 exactly and
unprompted, so `Track drain 30` now has a count it can trust between
readouts, which it never did before.


Guild.State
-----------

A delta feed - most packets carry one or two fields - so every read is
conditional and nothing is cleared by absence.

  repower_pct     WIRE-CONFIRMED to be the prompt's r%. Session log line
                  667 sends repower_pct 47; the prompt at line 718 reads
                  Status[w125%|pON|vON|r47%]. It is also the `powers`
                  screen's "N will free in X%". Feeds self.vars["reset"],
                  live, instead of only when a prompt happens to print.
  np              -> gp1, same as the text prompt's NP[]. No maximum is
                  sent; the prompt remains the source for npmax.
  globes_freeing  the "N will free" count. Observed, not consumed.
  hp_gain         per-tick hp delta, ranging -130..+55. UNCONSUMED - its
                  purpose is not established.
  reagents        all eight, ONE packet in the capture. Keys are singular
                  and underscored where `gs` prints them plural and spaced,
                  so necro.GMCP_REAGENTS maps them explicitly rather than by
                  a transform: spider_web -> "spider web" needs no plural,
                  black_pearl -> "black pearls" does, and only a table tells
                  them apart. Unknown keys are DROPPED, never guessed, so a
                  mud-side rename shows as a missing reagent rather than a
                  bogus tracker row. Deliberately NOT fed into the
                  `Reagents` restock command: that clears its buffer, sends
                  `gs` and buys target-have on a timer, so a second writer
                  with an unknown trigger (ONE reagents packet in a 30-tick
                  capture) could overwrite fresh counts with staler ones and
                  spend the difference. `gs` stays its only source.


Guild.Undead
------------

{corpses: N}, pushed on change. Matches the `inv` footer's trailing Nc and
the prompt's NP[..|Nc]: the capture sent corpses 1 seconds after the Syrup
Guard kill, against the player's own `Encumberance [368/2000|18%|  1c]`.

GAME FACT (user, 2026-09-03): necros preserve corpses to make them
lightweight and non-rotting, then consume them to restore hp/sp/np. The
`inv` footer's last two lines are encumbrance and the carried-corpse count.
