                         KNIGHTS GUILD: GMCP DATA FEED

GMCP ("Generic MUD Communication Protocol") lets a mud client receive your
guild information as structured data instead of screen text, for building
HUDs, overlays and third-party tools. It is entirely optional -- everything
still works exactly the same for you if your client does not use it.

If your client supports GMCP, subscribe with:

    Core.Supports.Set ["Guild 1"]

That single line turns on all eight of the guild's data packages below. A
client that wants only one can ask for it by name instead, e.g.
Core.Supports.Set ["Guild.State 1"], but there is no way to list the
package names from inside the protocol itself -- this help file, and the
comment block at the top of gobj/includes/gmcp.h in the mudlib
source, are the only places they are written down.

The eight packages:

  Guild.State       Your live HUD row -- stamina, strain, second wind,
                     stance, morale, guild level, class, banners, mount
                     status. Updates every game beat.
  Guild.Techniques  Your specials and protections, with whether each
                     is currently available and enabled. Updates every
                     few seconds.
  Guild.Info        Your order, fellowship rank, teachers, guild age,
                     suspensions and channel blocks -- what gs2/gs3 show.
                     Guild level and class live in Guild.State, not here.
  Guild.Progress    Your guild-experience economy -- gxp, bonuses,
                     the bury-donation tier -- what gs/gs2/gs3 show.
  Guild.Stats       Your combat statistics -- what cstat/cs2/
                     cs3 show.
  Guild.Assets      Charges and cooldowns -- sharpening stones,
                     esquires, jousts, campfire, oaths, shine. Your
                     banner count lives in Guild.State, not here.
  Guild.Skills      Every skill from the skills command, with level
                     and whether it is unlocked.
  Guild.Honors      Every honor from the honors command.

A package only sends data when something in it changes; if nothing has
changed, no message arrives at all. Silence means nothing changed -- it
does not mean your connection dropped or your client missed something.

The first message for a package after you subscribe (or after you
reconnect) carries its whole current picture. Every message after that
carries only the fields that changed since the last one. Your client must
keep its own copy of each package and update it field by field as these
messages arrive -- never throw away the fields a message did not mention;
they simply have not changed.

If you go idle for more than twenty minutes, your guild stops sending any
data at all until you act again -- the same rule that pauses your guild
heartbeat generally, not something specific to GMCP.

None of this changes anything about how the guild looks or plays if your
client does not use it. See knight abilities, knight general and
knight combat for the guild as everyone sees it.
